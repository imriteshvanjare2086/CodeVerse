# CodeVerse

**Your coding progress, connected.**

CodeVerse is the hackathon edition of CodeTrack—a platform that brings your coding profiles, learning goals and personalized guidance together.

## What you can do

- **Track your progress** across LeetCode, Codeforces and CodeChef.
- **Get coding help** from CodeT, powered by Gemma 4.
- **Discover personalized recommendations** based on your coding stats and contest history.
- **Compare performance** with friends in Battle Arena.
- **Stay motivated** with goals, achievements and leaderboards.
- **Keep learning** with DSA sheets, courses, roadmaps and contest information.

Built to help you practice consistently, understand your progress and choose what to learn next.
